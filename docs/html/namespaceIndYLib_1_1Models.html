record IndYLib.Models.DayStatus ( [property:JsonPropertyName("date")] DateOnly Date,
[property:JsonPropertyName("day_name")] string DayName,
[property:JsonPropertyName("status")] Status Status )

The status of a indy day.

Parameters
DateThe date of the indy day.
DayNameThe name of the indy day. ('Mo', 'Mi', 'Fr')
StatusThe actual status.
  • 0: No entries have been made yet, can still be made.
  • 1: idk (i haven't found a 1 status yet).
  • 2: At least 1 entry has been made, but not signed yet.
  • 3: Both Entries have been made and signed.
  • 4: At least 1 entry has not been made and cannot be still made. Or teacher is absent.
  • 5: New special indy has been made for this teacher and thus the entry has been cancelled.
  • 6: Absence entries have been made.
